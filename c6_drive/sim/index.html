The original interactive experiments are kept below. They use the old browser model; the results of this round's C++ candidate controllers are above and are not recomputed when you move the sliders here.

The motor model comes from identification with the wheels off the ground. Robot weight, thrust, friction and adhesion are adjustable assumptions; the simulated position sensor does not mean the real robot has one. Low speed and holding on the slope are model extrapolations.
