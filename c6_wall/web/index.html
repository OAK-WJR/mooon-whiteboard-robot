Manual control

W should drive toward the robot front. If it drives off at an angle, adjust "Front correction" (counterclockwise is positive), then click "Try W" to check. Releasing the keys stops smoothly and locks the position; losing the network does the same. Only "Release" removes the holding force (brake state).

Erase board

Put the robot in the start corner (leave a margin from the body center to the board edge), front facing up, click "Enable", then "Set origin". "Drivable width/height" is the range the body center can travel.

Slip compensation (for wall use) Off

Encoders cannot see slip. Measure once how far the robot actually moved, and it will then drive more or less to compensate. When testing on the floor, click "Restore defaults".

Measurement helper

① Mark the body center with tape ② Click a test direction ③ Once stopped, measure the actual distance moved (for sideways tests, also measure the sag and front rotation) ④ Click "Update from measurement".
